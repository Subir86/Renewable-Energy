Biomass Energy

What is Biomass Energy?

Biomass energy is renewable energy produced from organic materials such as plants, wood, animal waste, and agricultural waste.

Advantages of Biomass Energy

  • Uses organic and agricultural waste.
  • Reduces waste pollution.
  • Can produce electricity and heat.
  • Is a renewable source of energy.

Disadvantages

  • Burning biomass can produce smoke and carbon emissions.
  • Large-scale biomass production requires land and resources.
