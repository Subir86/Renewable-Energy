Solar Energy

What is Solar Energy?

Solar energy is the energy obtained from the Sun. It is a clean and renewable source of energy. Solar panels use sunlight to generate electricity for homes, schools, industries and many other places.

How Does Solar Energy Work?

Solar panels contain photovoltaic cells. When sunlight falls on these cells, solar energy is converted into electrical energy.

Advantages of Solar Energy

  • It is a renewable source of energy.
  • It produces clean electricity.
  • It reduces pollution.
  • It reduces dependence on fossil fuels.
  • It can be installed on rooftops.
