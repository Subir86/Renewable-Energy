Wind Energy

What is Wind Energy?

Wind energy is a renewable source of energy that uses the natural movement of air to generate electricity. Wind turbines convert the kinetic energy of moving wind into electrical energy.

How Does Wind Energy Work?

Wind turbines have large blades that rotate when wind blows. These rotating blades turn a generator, which converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.

Advantages of Wind Energy

  • It is a clean and renewable source of energy.
  • It does not produce harmful air pollution.
  • It reduces dependence on fossil fuels.
  • It helps reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Wind is naturally available and free.
